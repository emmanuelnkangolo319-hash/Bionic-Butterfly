Now, i will explain the math:

We used all the base formulas in aerodynamics, for lift, drag and thrust. All we did was find out, thanks to many sources, that the main difference is the changes in each variable. For example, the coefficient of lift and drag are relatively stable on normal aircraft, but they constantly change when we bring flapping wings into the mix, we a just researched whats their range for our specific project (for bionic butterflys its from around 2.0-2,5 depending on the speed) and basically made it oscilate between those 2 values, trying to simulate the real life changes, like wind speed of life just being a pain in the private.

We are left with the best parts of this project, building and testing, i will add what we discover and what we intergrate into the project after this phase.
